What We Do

Key Responsibilities

Academic Planning & Coordination

  • Preparing the university academic calendar for all programmes
  • Creating teaching and consultation timetables each semester
  • Preparing examination timetables for all courses
  • Coordinating with Deans and Heads of Departments
  • Ensuring teaching and examination schedules are observed
  • Coordinating lecture halls, labs and workshops
  • Scheduling academic field trips
  • Managing timetable conflicts and resource allocation

Examinations & Quality Assurance

  • Coordinating the processing and conduct of examinations
  • Managing venue logistics and invigilation assignments
  • Ensuring examination integrity and confidentiality
  • Facilitating special examination arrangements
  • Supporting Deans and HODs for curriculum delivery
  • Facilitating external examiner involvement and visits
  • Maintaining records and statutory compliance
  • Supporting audit and accreditation requirements

DR

DR
