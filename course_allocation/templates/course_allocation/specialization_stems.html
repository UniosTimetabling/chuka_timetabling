Specialization Stems are different from Selection Groups. A student picks exactly ONE stem (e.g. "Artificial Intelligence") out of a category and then takes ALL the courses in that stem together. Courses inside the SAME stem must never clash with each other. Courses that belong to DIFFERENT stems of the SAME category may safely share a timeslot, since a student only ever follows one stem.
