Split into sections

Use this when a course has too many students for one class. The new sections stay in the same student group / stem / elective group as this course. Sections share the same students' cohort, so the schedulers may place them at the same time (different rooms) — each section still clashes with everything else the course would clash with.

Map to Student Group(s)

Tick every student group that takes this unit. One unit can serve several groups — no Combined Course Group needed. Two units of the same course (e.g. EDCI 111A and EDCI 111B) can be mapped to the same groups: those groups' students are then split between the two units, so one unit may hold just Group 1 while the other holds Group 1 plus others. The units may be scheduled at the same time; each still clashes with the other courses of every group it holds.

Elective groups in stem

Tick a pick-one group to nest it in this stem. Example: the stem has 12 units, 10 are core and the student chooses ONE of the last two — make those two a group and tick it here. The group's courses become members of the stem (they still clash with the 10 core units), but the alternatives may share a time slot with each other.
